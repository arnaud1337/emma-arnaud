    Welcome

    We are so happy to have you with us for this very special weekend.

    After almost ten years of building our life together, we are delighted to bring you all together to celebrate our wedding in a place that is dear to us, in our beautiful Île-de-France region.

    We imagined these few days as a little pause to share with the people who matter most to us: a little ceremony, a lot of fun, music, long tables, laughter and, we hope, a few memories we will treasure for a long time.

    The wedding will begin in Paris on Friday, then continue on Saturday and Sunday at L’Orangerie du Moulin, in Bransles.

    We created this website so you can easily find everything you need to plan your trip: the weekend schedule, how to get there, where to stay, good addresses and a few ideas for extending your stay in the area.

    We can’t wait to see you and share all of this with you.

    See you very soon,
    Emma & Arnaud

    Good to know

    Practical information

    How do I get to L’Orangerie du Moulin?

    We recommend coming by car, so you can make the most of the weekend and easily reach your accommodation. From Paris, allow about 1 hr 15 min via the A6 motorway.

    You can also come from Paris by train (Transilien line R from Gare de Lyon to Dordives station, then a 15-minute bike ride or a 50-minute walk).

    Where should I stay?

    We are planning to book holiday cottages (gîtes) near the wedding venue. If you would like us to help you arrange your accommodation, just get in touch. You are also welcome to book your own accommodation directly.

    Plan your stay: where to sleep?
    Can we bring our children?

    We adore your children, but unfortunately the wedding venue is not suitable for them, as there are many unfenced streams and waterways on the grounds. If you are unable to come without your children, please get in touch with us: Emma · Arnaud · by e-mail.

    Is there a dress code?

    We would like the wedding to be elegant but relaxed, celebrating colourful, country-style outfits. Above all, wear something you feel good in — and that you’ll want to dance in until the very end of the night!

    Will there be parking?

    Yes, secure parking will be available, with an attendant on site during the evening.

    What about dietary preferences and requirements?

    You can let us know about any allergies or dietary requirements in the RSVP form.

    Can we give you a gift?

    Having you with us will be the most wonderful gift. But if you would still like to contribute to our honeymoon, you will be able to do so through our online honeymoon fund.

    How do I get back to my accommodation after the party on Saturday?

    So that you can enjoy the party without a care, we are planning a shuttle service to take you back to your accommodation nearby. Just let us know if you would like to use it!

    Places

      A few well-chosen detours

      Extend your stay

      If you would like to make the most of your trip to get to know the area a little better, you are in the right place.

      With its forests, characterful villages, châteaux and pretty riverside towns, the south of Île-de-France and the area around Bransles have more than enough to fill a long weekend.

      Château de Fontainebleau

      A heritage outing well worth considering, combining architecture, gardens and history.

      Fontainebleau Forest

      For walking, breathing deeply and enjoying some time surrounded by nature.

      Moret-sur-Loing

      A riverside stop, to explore at a leisurely pace.

      Barbizon

      A characterful artists’ village to add to your walks as you please.

      Château-Landon

      Old streets and a real village atmosphere to discover.

      Nemours

      A nearby town rich in heritage, to explore at your own pace.

      Loing Canal

      An idea for a peaceful stroll along the water.

      Château de Rosa Bonheur

      Visit, by reservation, the home, studio and works of the artist Rosa Bonheur.
